Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LemonMusic (the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our AI music discovery, streaming, social, download, and local playback features.

By using LemonMusic, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by law, we will ask for it separately before collecting or using the relevant information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, login verification information, and, if you choose Google sign-in, the basic account information authorized by you and provided by Google.
  • Profile information: your nickname, profile image, and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • User content: comments, replies, reports, feedback, playlist names, playlists, likes, and other content or actions you submit in the App.
  • Support information: the information included in messages, feedback, reports, or other communications you send to us.

1.2 Music Activity and Preferences

We may collect information about how you use LemonMusic, including:

  • songs, artists, playlists, and modes you play, view, or search for;
  • playback history, listening duration, skips, repeats, likes, dislikes, “Not Interested” choices, shares, and downloads;
  • playlists you create and content you add to or remove from them;
  • recommendation interactions, selected moods, genres, scenes, and listening modes; and
  • remaining free-listening time, rewarded-ad status, membership status, and subscription entitlement status.

If you use LemonMusic before signing in, some listening activity may be stored on your device and linked to your account after you sign in so that your history and preferences can be synchronized.

1.3 Device, Network, and Technical Information

We may automatically collect:

  • device model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, country or general region inferred from network information, and network type;
  • IP address, device or app identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, and notification token;
  • app launches, feature interactions, session information, diagnostics, crash logs, and performance data; and
  • ad impressions, ad interactions, rewarded-ad completion, and related attribution or fraud-prevention information.

We do not use GPS or precise location solely to recommend music. We may use general region, language, time zone, and local time to provide relevant content and order listening modes.

1.4 Device Permissions and Local Music

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we may send service updates, music recommendations, membership information, or other notices. You can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Photos or media: if you choose a profile image, the App may request access to the photo or media you select.
  • Audio files and media library: if you use Local Music, the App may request permission to find, display, organize, and play supported audio files on your device. This access is used for the Local Music feature and not to personalize advertising.
  • Storage: if you download music, the App may store and manage downloaded files on your device.

You may refuse or revoke optional permissions through your device settings. Some related features may then be unavailable.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide music playback, search, downloads, playlists, comments, sharing, local music, and account synchronization;
  • personalize songs, playlists, AI artists, modes, and recommendations based on your preferences and activity;
  • maintain your profile, likes, playlists, listening history, free-time rewards, and membership benefits across supported devices;
  • process rewarded advertising, measure advertising performance, limit fraud, and provide ad-funded listening access;
  • verify subscription status and provide paid features such as unlimited listening, ad-free listening, downloads, and higher audio quality;
  • moderate comments, respond to reports, protect copyright, enforce our rules, and prevent abuse, spam, fraud, or security incidents;
  • analyze and improve App reliability, playback quality, content, recommendation performance, and user experience;
  • communicate with you and respond to support requests; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect legal rights.

3. Advertising, Analytics, and Purchases

LemonMusic may provide rewarded or other advertisements. Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and partner settings, ads may be contextual or personalized. Advertising and attribution providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, general region, ad impressions, interactions, and reward completion.

We may use analytics and crash-reporting providers to understand App usage and improve stability. These providers may process device, diagnostic, and usage information on our behalf or under their own privacy policies.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the relevant app store or payment provider. We generally receive transaction identifiers, purchase status, subscription status, product information, country, and limited billing-related metadata rather than your full payment card details.

You can limit ad personalization through your device privacy or advertising settings and, where available, through the App’s consent controls. Changing these settings may not reduce the number of ads you receive.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: with vendors that support hosting, authentication, email verification, content delivery, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, moderation, security, advertising, attribution, and payment or subscription verification.
  • Public and social features: your nickname, profile image, comments, replies, and related engagement may be visible to other users. When you use your device’s sharing function, information is sent to the app or person you select under their terms and privacy practices.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, enforce our terms, investigate fraud, protect users, defend legal claims, or protect rights, property, and safety.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to applicable law.
  • With your direction or consent: when you ask us to share information or otherwise authorize the sharing.

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain disclosures to advertising partners may be considered “sharing,” “targeted advertising,” or a “sale” under some privacy laws. Where applicable, we provide the choices required by law.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the App, maintaining your account, complying with law, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, preventing fraud, and protecting security. The retention period depends on the type of information, the feature for which it is used, and legal requirements.

You may request deletion of your LemonMusic account and associated personal information through the account controls available in the App or by using the in-app Feedback feature. We will verify and process eligible requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may be retained where legally required or permitted, such as transaction, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute records.

Uninstalling the App removes App data stored locally by the App, subject to your operating system and backup settings, but it does not by itself delete information associated with a registered account. Downloaded or local audio files remain subject to your device’s storage controls.

6. Security and International Data Transfers

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including protections for information transmitted between the App and our services. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

LemonMusic and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

7. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or appeal a decision concerning your request. You may also have the right to opt out of targeted advertising, sharing, or sales as those terms are defined by applicable law.

You can manage certain information and choices in the App or through your device settings. To submit another privacy request, use the in-app Feedback feature and clearly state that your request is privacy-related. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You will not be discriminated against for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Children’s Privacy

LemonMusic is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in their country to consent to the processing of personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age without authorization required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of this section, please contact us through the in-app Feedback feature so we can take appropriate action.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may contain links to or integrations with third-party services, such as sign-in providers, app stores, sharing destinations, advertisers, and external websites. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to LemonMusic, our data practices, or legal requirements. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last Updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, privacy requests, or account-deletion requests, contact the LemonMusic support team through the in-app Feedback feature.
